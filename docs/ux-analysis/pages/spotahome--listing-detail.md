## Observed principles

- **First viewport contract:** photos + trust + a date form. **No price** —
  the card says "Select dates to see prices"; the number hides 5,900px down
  in §Pricing. The page trades transparency for lead capture.
- **Trust is the product:** four separate trust surfaces (rail, main-col
  repeat, cancellation, how-to-book) — more pixels than the property's own
  description. The marketplace sells *safety of transacting*, the flat is
  almost secondary.
- **Reading order enforced:** the sticky anchor bar names 7 stations —
  the page knows it is too long (7,800px) to be scanned unaided.
- **Platform voice over owner voice:** "Spotahome's opinion" is
  platform-written and translated; the landlord's text is shown raw
  (Spanish on the EN page). Verification of *content*, not just existence.
- **Process pedagogy:** a 4-step "How to book" on every listing — the booking
  model (pay before landlord accepts) is unusual enough to need teaching.
- **Social proof as urgency:** views/favourites/interested counters — the
  only mapped site that shows demand signals on the listing.
- **Map demoted** to a gallery tab; neighbourhood barely exists as content
  (auto-generated opinion lists a few POIs).
