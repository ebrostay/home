## Observed principles

- **Same honest unit as Ebrostay:** "avg …€ / 30 days" — the only
  competitor pricing in 30-day units, plus "incl. utilities and internet"
  right under the number, deposit 0 € stated in-page.
- **Payment-timeline transparency exists here too** — but hidden behind a
  "When will I pay what?" link and dates; Ebrostay's inline schedule is
  still stronger, no longer unique in kind.
- **The host is a person:** avatar on the description, tenure, rating,
  response time, approval rate, "send question". Flatio sells a
  relationship where Blueground sells an operation.
- **Digital-nomad lens:** internet speed as a section, unit-purpose tag,
  visa-friendly lease, street-view + travel times. The audience is encoded
  in the information architecture.
- **Urgency theater** (seen-by counter, "soon be gone") sits directly under
  the price — the one discordant note in an otherwise reassuring rail.
- **Longest page, weakest hierarchy:** 17 stations and no anchor nav; the
  platform sections (5 reasons, FAQ, StayProtection ×2 surfaces) stretch the
  read; trust content again outweighs home content below the fold.
